# YouTube script / Сценарий для YouTube

Brief: $ARGUMENTS

Write in the channel's language (default: the user's).

## Output

1. **Positioning:** viewer, their question, the payoff they get by the end.
2. **3 titles** (≤ 60 chars, concrete, no clickbait the video does not deliver) and **2 thumbnail ideas** (≤ 4 words of text, one emotion, one object).
3. **Intro (0:00–0:30):** promise the payoff, show a glimpse of it, say why to trust you.
4. **Chapters** with timestamps.
5. **Script** — per chapter: what is said, what is on screen (B-roll, screen capture, graphics), and a re-hook every 60–90 seconds.
6. **Ending:** recap in one line, one CTA, end-screen suggestion.
7. **Description** with timestamps, links and the sources of every fact used; **pinned comment**.

Facts, numbers and quotes need sources — list them. If the video is sponsored or uses an AI persona, include a clear disclosure line. Delegate content plans and series to the `content-producer` agent.

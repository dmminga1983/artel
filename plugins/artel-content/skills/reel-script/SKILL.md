# Reel script / Сценарий рилса

Brief: $ARGUMENTS

Write in the audience's language (default: the user's).

## Brief

Platform and length (default 20–35 s) · audience · one takeaway · CTA · persona (if any — load or create a persona sheet: name, niche, voice, catchphrases, look, banned topics) · what can be produced (filming, stock, AI video, screen recording).

## Output

1. **3 hooks** (first 1–2 s): each = visual + line, different angles (question, bold claim, "mistake" frame, before/after).
2. **Script table**

| Time | Visual | Voice / text on screen | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|

3. **Caption** with 3–5 relevant hashtags, and the pinned-comment text.
4. **Production notes:** shot list, props, prompts for AI image/video tools if used.
5. **Variation:** one alternative angle for A/B testing.

## Rules

One idea per video; captions on screen for silent viewing; a reason to rewatch or a loop. No invented statistics, fake reviews, or unsourced health/money/legal claims. Use licensed or original music and characters — not copyrighted ones. Delegate series and calendars to the `content-producer` agent.

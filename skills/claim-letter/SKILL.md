# Claim letter / Претензия и официальное письмо

Situation: $ARGUMENTS

Write in Russian unless asked otherwise.

## 1. Collect (ask only for what is missing)

Sender and addressee (full legal names, addresses, contract numbers and dates) · what happened, with dates · which contract clauses and documents prove it · what exactly is demanded (fix defects, pay an amount, reduce price, terminate) · deadline · how it will be sent (registered letter with a list of contents, e-mail per contract, by hand against signature).

## 2. Structure

1. Header: to whom, from whom, date, outgoing number.
2. Title: «Претензия», «Ответ на претензию от … № …», «Требование об устранении недостатков».
3. **Facts** — chronological, each with the document that proves it.
4. **Legal basis** — contract clauses and law articles. Every article is checked in the current text of the law before it is cited; if unsure, mark it for the lawyer.
5. **Calculation** of any amount — done in code, shown in a table.
6. **Demands** — numbered, specific, measurable, with a deadline.
7. **Consequences** if not met (court, penalties under the contract/law) — stated calmly, without threats.
8. **Attachments** list.
9. Signature block.

## 3. Reply to a claim

Go through the claimant's demands one by one: accept / partly accept / reject — each with grounds and documents. Offer a practical way forward when it is in the user's interest (inspection date, joint survey, remediation schedule).

## Rules

Only facts the documents support; no invented dates, amounts or articles. Neutral, business tone. Mark placeholders clearly: `[ДАТА]`, `[№ ДОГОВОРА]`. The user — or their lawyer — reviews before sending. Not legal advice.
